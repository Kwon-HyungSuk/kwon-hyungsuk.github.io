Ultrasound Sensing
My work in ultrasound sensing focuses on using acoustic and ultrasonic wave interactions to detect objects, resolve shapes, and infer material properties in complex environments. The research combines wave physics, metamaterial design, and machine learning to improve sensing reliability where conventional optical or electromagnetic approaches are limited.
Ultrasound Energy Transfer
This area explores efficient delivery of mechanical and electrical energy through ultrasonic waves, with emphasis on piezoelectric systems, resonant structures, and acoustic metamaterials. The goal is to enable compact and robust wireless power transfer in challenging settings, including through layered or non-ideal media.
Underwater Acoustics
Underwater acoustics addresses how acoustic signals propagate, scatter, and interact in marine environments. My research in this direction aims to develop acoustic methods for communication, sensing, and energy transfer in underwater systems where electromagnetic signals are ineffective or impractical.
